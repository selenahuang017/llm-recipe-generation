**LUNCH**

1. **Title** – Duck & Ramen Stir‑Fry with Orange‑Glazed Potatoes  
2. **Serving size** – 1 person  
3. **Ingredients & amounts**  
   - 5 oz sliced duck breast (≈ 300 cal)  
   - 1.5 cups dry gluten‑free ramen noodles (≈ 450 cal)  
   - 1 medium potato, diced (≈ 110 cal)  
   - 1/2 orange – juice & zest (≈ 45 cal)  
   - 1 tsp olive oil (≈ 40 cal)  
   - 1 tbsp gluten‑free soy sauce (≈ 10 cal)  
   - 1 tsp honey (≈ 20 cal)  
   - Salt & pepper to taste  
4. **Instructions**  
   1. Cook ramen according to package instructions (using gluten‑free noodles). Drain and set aside.  
   2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium‑high heat. Add duck breast slices, searing until browned and cooked through (~ 4–5 min). Remove and slice into bite‑sized pieces.  
   3. In the same skillet, toss diced potato, cooking until just tender and lightly crisp (≈ 7 min).  
   4. Return duck to the skillet, add the cooked ramen, orange juice & zest, soy sauce, and honey. Stir everything together, letting the sauce coat the noodles and potatoes (≈ 2 min).  
   5. Season with salt & pepper, serve hot.  
5. **Nutritional information** – Approx. 945 kcal (protein ≈ 32 g, fat ≈ 32 g, carbs ≈ 120 g).  
6. **Anything else** – Uses gluten‑free ramen; all other ingredients are naturally gluten‑free.

**DINNER**

1. **Title** – Roasted Duck Thigh with Orange‑Glazed Potatoes  
2. **Serving size** – 1 person  
3. **Ingredients & amounts**  
   - 5 oz duck thigh, bone‑in (≈ 300 cal)  
   - 1 medium potato, cubed (≈ 110 cal)  
   - 1/2 orange – juice & zest (≈ 45 cal)  
   - 1 tsp olive oil (≈ 40 cal)  
   - 1 tsp honey (≈ 20 cal)  
   - Salt, pepper, and a pinch of smoked paprika  
4. **Instructions**  
   1. Preheat the oven to 375 °F (190 °C).  
   2. Toss potato cubes with olive oil, salt, pepper, and paprika; spread on a baking sheet.  
   3. Place the duck thigh skin‑side up on the sheet and roast for 25–30 min, until the skin is crisp and the meat reaches an internal temperature of 165 °F (74 °C).  
   4. While the duck roasts, whisk orange juice, zest, and honey together.  
   5. Once the duck is done, remove from the oven and brush the orange glaze over the skin. Return to the oven for an additional 5 min to caramelize the glaze.  
   6. Serve the roasted duck alongside the glazed potatoes.  
5. **Nutritional information** – Approx. 480 kcal (protein ≈ 24 g, fat ≈ 28 g, carbs ≈ 35 g).  
6. **Anything else** – Entire dish is gluten‑free; no wheat, barley, or rye.
