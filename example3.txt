## BREAKFAST  
**1. Rhubarb‑Persimmon Yogurt Parfait with Oreo Crumble & Sesame Seeds**  

**2. Serving Size** – 1 parfait (serves 1)  

**3. Ingredients & Amounts**  
| Ingredient | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| Fresh rhubarb, chopped | ½ cup (≈70 g) |
| Fresh persimmon, diced | ½ cup (≈60 g) |
| Gluten‑free Oreos (8 pieces) | 1/2 cup crumbled (≈30 g) |
| Toasted sesame seeds | 1 Tbsp (≈10 g) |
| Plain Greek yogurt (non‑fat) | 1 cup (≈240 g) |
| Honey (optional) | 1 tsp |
| Pinch of sea salt | – |

**4. Instructions**  
1. In a small bowl, combine the crumbled Oreos and toasted sesame seeds.  
2. In a serving glass, layer ¼ cup Greek yogurt, ¼ cup rhubarb, ¼ cup persimmon, and a sprinkle of the Oreo‑sesame mix.  
3. Repeat the layers once more.  
4. Drizzle honey over the top (if using) and add a pinch of salt.  
5. Chill for 10 min before serving.  

**5. Nutritional Information (per parfait)**  
- Calories: **230 kcal**  
- Protein: **11 g**  
- Fat: **4 g** (mostly heart‑healthy monounsaturated from sesame)  
- Carbohydrate: **37 g** (including 6 g sugar)  
- Fiber: **4 g**  

**6. Anything else**  
- The parfait is 100 % gluten‑free (use the certified gluten‑free Oreo line).  
- Swap honey for agave or a low‑GI sweetener if you’re watching carbs.

## LUNCH  
**1. Sesame‑Crusted Duck Breast with Tomato Salsa over Brown Rice**  

**2. Serving Size** – 1 plate (serves 1)  

**3. Ingredients & Amounts**  
| Ingredient | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| Duck breast (bone‑in) | 6 oz (≈170 g) |
| Toasted sesame seeds | 2 Tbsp (≈20 g) |
| Ripe tomato, diced | 1 cup (≈150 g) |
| Red onion, minced | 2 Tbsp |
| Fresh cilantro, chopped | 1 Tbsp |
| Lime juice | 1 Tbsp |
| Brown rice, cooked | 1 cup (≈195 g) |
| Olive oil | 1 tsp |
| Salt & pepper | to taste |

**4. Instructions**  
1. Preheat oven to 375 °F (190 °C).  
2. Pat the duck breast dry; season with salt and pepper.  
3. Press toasted sesame seeds onto the skin side of the breast.  
4. Heat a skillet over medium‑high heat, add 1 tsp olive oil, sear the duck skin side for 4 min until golden.  
5. Transfer the skillet to the oven and roast 10 min, or until internal temp reaches 135 °F (57 °C) for medium‑rare.  
6. Let the duck rest 5 min, then slice thinly.  
7. In a bowl, combine diced tomato, onion, cilantro, and lime juice; season with salt.  
8. Serve sliced duck over a bed of brown rice, topped with tomato salsa.  

**5. Nutritional Information (per plate)**  
- Calories: **420 kcal**  
- Protein: **35 g**  
- Fat: **18 g** (primarily healthy fats from duck & sesame)  
- Carbohydrate: **32 g**  
- Fiber: **5 g**  

**6. Anything else**  
- This dish is naturally gluten‑free.  
- If you prefer a crispier skin, broil the duck for 2 min after roasting.

## DINNER  
**1. Persimmon & Tomato Duck Stir‑Fry over Sesame Rice**  

**2. Serving Size** – 1 bowl (serves 1)  

**3. Ingredients & Amounts**  
| Ingredient | Amount |
|------------|--------|
| Leftover duck breast, sliced | 6 oz (≈170 g) |
| Fresh persimmon, diced | ½ cup (≈60 g) |
| Ripe tomato, diced | ½ cup (≈75 g) |
| Garlic, minced | 1 clove |
| Fresh ginger, grated | ½ tsp |
| Tamari (gluten‑free soy sauce) | 1 Tbsp |
| Sesame oil | 1 tsp |
| Cooked jasmine rice | 1 cup (≈195 g) |
| Toasted sesame seeds | 1 tsp |
| Green onion, sliced (optional) | 1 Tbsp |

**4. Instructions**  
1. Heat sesame oil in a wok or large skillet over medium‑high heat.  
2. Add garlic and ginger; stir for 30 s until fragrant.  
3. Toss in sliced duck, cooking until the edges start to crisp (≈3 min).  
4. Add persimmon and tomato; cook 2 min more, just until the fruit softens but still holds shape.  
5. Stir in tamari and a splash of water (≈2 Tbsp) to create a quick sauce.  
6. While the stir‑fry finishes, reheat jasmine rice and sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds.  
7. Plate the rice, spoon the duck stir‑fry over it, and garnish with green onion if desired.  

**5. Nutritional Information (per bowl)**  
- Calories: **385 kcal**  
- Protein: **31 g**  
- Fat: **12 g** (good sources of omega‑3 from duck)  
- Carbohydrate: **29 g**  
- Fiber: **4 g**  

**6. Anything else**  
- All ingredients are gluten‑free; tamari replaces regular soy sauce.  
- Feel free to add a dash of sriracha for heat.
