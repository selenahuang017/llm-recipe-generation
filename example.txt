## BREAKFAST  

**1. Rhubarb‑Persimmon Rice Porridge with Sesame Seeds & Gluten‑Free Oreos**  
**2. Serving size** – 1 bowl (≈ 280 mL)  
**3. Ingredients**  
| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
|------------|--------|-------|
| White rice (cooked) | ½ cup (≈ 100 g) | Use pre‑cooked or quick‑cooking rice |
| Rhubarb | ¼ cup diced (≈ 35 g) | Fresh or frozen |
| Persimmon | ¼ cup diced (≈ 35 g) | Fresh, peeled |
| Sesame seeds | 1 Tbsp | Toasted or raw |
| Gluten‑free Oreos | 3 pieces | Crushed lightly |
| Maple syrup | 1 Tbsp | Sweetener |
| Salt | Pinch |  |

**4. Instructions**  
1. In a small saucepan, heat ½ cup water until it just boils. Add the diced rhubarb and persimmon and simmer for 3‑4 min until slightly softened.  
2. Stir in the cooked rice and continue to cook for another 2 min, allowing the flavors to mingle.  
3. Remove from heat, stir in maple syrup and a pinch of salt.  
4. Pour the porridge into a bowl, sprinkle with sesame seeds and gently fold in the crushed Oreos for a crunchy texture.  
5. Serve warm, optionally with a side of fresh fruit.

**5. Nutritional information (per serving)**  
- Calories: ~400 kcal  
- Protein: 7 g  
- Fat: 12 g (mostly from sesame seeds)  
- Carbohydrates: 70 g  
- Fiber: 4 g  
- Sugar: 25 g (naturally occurring + maple syrup)

**6. Anything else**  
- **Gluten‑free** – All ingredients are certified gluten‑free.  
- Use a non‑stick pan or a small pot to avoid sticking.

## LUNCH  

**1. Duck & Tomato Rice Stir‑Fry with Sesame**  
**2. Serving size** – 1 plate (≈ 300 mL)  
**3. Ingredients**  
| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
|------------|--------|-------|
| Duck breast, sliced | 100 g | Trim fat if desired |
| Tomatoes, diced | 1 cup (≈ 150 g) | Fresh or canned (no added salt) |
| Cooked white rice | ½ cup (≈ 100 g) | Leftover or freshly cooked |
| Sesame seeds | 1 Tbsp | Toasted for extra flavor |
| Tamari (gluten‑free soy sauce) | 1 tsp | Use a small bottle |
| Garlic powder | ½ tsp | Optional |
| Oil (vegetable or sesame) | 1 Tbsp | For sautéing |
| Salt & pepper | pinch each | To taste |

**4. Instructions**  
1. Heat oil in a non‑stick skillet over medium heat. Add duck slices and sear for 3–4 min on each side until cooked through and golden. Remove duck and set aside.  
2. In the same skillet, add diced tomatoes and garlic powder; sauté until the tomatoes break down (≈2 min).  
3. Return the duck to the skillet, then stir in cooked rice and tamari. Cook for another 2 min, ensuring everything is heated evenly.  
4. Sprinkle toasted sesame seeds over the stir‑fry and give a final toss.  
5. Plate the mixture, garnish with a few extra sesame seeds, and serve hot.

**5. Nutritional information (per serving)**  
- Calories: ~500 kcal  
- Protein: 25 g  
- Fat: 28 g (duck + oil)  
- Carbohydrates: 35 g  
- Fiber: 4 g  

**6. Anything else**  
- **Gluten‑free** – Replace regular soy sauce with tamari.  
- Use a high‑heat oil to avoid smoking.

## DINNER  

**1. Persimmon‑Glazed Duck with Tomato Sauce over Rice**  
**2. Serving size** – 1 plate (≈ 350 mL)  
**3. Ingredients**  
| Ingredient | Amount | Notes |
|------------|--------|-------|
| Duck leg (bone‑in) | 150 g | Clean and pat dry |
| Persimmon | 1 medium, sliced into thin rings (≈ 75 g) | For glaze |
| Tomato sauce (no added salt) | ½ cup (≈ 125 g) | Low‑sodium, gluten‑free |
| Cooked white rice | ½ cup (≈ 100 g) | As a side |
| Sesame seeds | 1 tsp | Toasted |
| Olive oil | 1 Tbsp | For searing |
| Salt & pepper | pinch each | To taste |
| Optional sugar | 1 tsp | For sweeter glaze |

**4. Instructions**  
1. Preheat oven to 180 °C (350 °F). Season the duck leg with salt and pepper.  
2. Heat olive oil in an oven‑safe skillet over medium‑high heat. Sear the duck, skin‑side down, until browned (≈4 min). Flip and sear the other side for 2 min.  
3. Transfer the skillet to the oven and roast for 20 min or until the internal temperature reaches 70 °C (160 °F).  
4. While the duck rests, blend persimmon slices with 1 tsp sugar (if using) to create a quick puree. Warm this in a small saucepan for 2 min; this will be your glaze.  
5. Warm the tomato sauce in a saucepan for 3 min, adding a pinch of pepper.  
6. Slice the duck leg, drizzle with the persimmon glaze, then spoon tomato sauce over it. Serve atop the cooked rice, garnished with toasted sesame seeds.  

**5. Nutritional information (per serving)**  
- Calories: ~600 kcal  
- Protein: 35 g  
- Fat: 35 g (duck + oil)  
- Carbohydrates: 45 g  
- Fiber: 5 g  

**6. Anything else**  
- **Gluten‑free** – Ensure tomato sauce is labeled gluten‑free.  
- Adjust oven time if using a different cut or thickness of duck.
